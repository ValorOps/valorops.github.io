| 01 Materials (2026-11-19) | Can glass, metal and grain carry a ValorOps page without reading as decoration? When is frosted glass earned? | Graphite (#0b0e11 / #141a1f), ink #e8edf1, signal orange #ff6b2c. Archivo variable, Geist, Geist Mono. | Glass earns its place when it hides something and the visitor removes it: frost over deliverables, a lens to look through, Inspect to clear. Brushed metal reads as real from a CSS stack (stretched SVG noise in overlay blend, hairline gradient, diagonal sheen, top and bottom edge light, screw heads). One fixed SVG grain overlay at 5% overlay blend. Perf (headless Chrome, 390x844, CPU 4x, lens sweeping): glass median 16.7ms p95 16.8ms, 46% of frames over 16.7ms; no-backdrop median 16.7ms p95 16.8ms, 40%. No measurable jank; real phone not yet checked. | Noise as a layer under soft-light on a near-transparent element reads as TV static: put grain in a pseudo-element at 5 to 8% instead. A 2px-tall stretched noise strip repeats into vertical streaks: stretch a 3200x160 tile. `page.addStyleTag` with inline CSS is blocked by style-src 'self': tests must inject same-origin stylesheets. On phones nothing tells you to tap the frost; only the Inspect button signals it. | Frost lens: `.frost` absolute over the content, backdrop-filter blur(14px) saturate(1.3), mask-image radial hole at var(--lx, -999px) var(--ly, -999px), 1px border at 45% ink, rim element translated to the same point; JS sets --lx/--ly per rAF from pointermove (fine pointer only) and pointerdown (touch), Inspect toggles aria-pressed + .is-clear; html.no-backdrop makes the frost opaque. Metal plate: see .plate in lab/01-materials/site.css. | Pending Dillan's live review. Candidate rule: "Glass is allowed when it conceals and the visitor clears it; never as a resting surface for text. Needs a 3:1 border, an opaque fallback, and a non-pointer way to clear it." |

**01 review (web-review pass, desktop/768/390/360 rendered), second-pass candidates, not yet fixed:**
1. Must fix before reuse on valor-ops.com: the frost hides the deliverables, which are purchase-deciding information, and on phones nothing signals the pane is tappable. Glass should conceal secondary detail (evidence, methodology), never what the visitor is deciding on. Add an in-glass hint on touch or move the frost to the sample finding.
2. Should fix: "Inspect" does not say what happens ("Show what you receive"), and the pill buttons break the card radius scale (14px cards, 999px buttons). Use one radius family.
3. Should fix: large prices in Geist Mono read as "$1 , 500" because of the monospaced comma. Set big amounts in Archivo with tabular-nums; keep mono for durations and labels.
