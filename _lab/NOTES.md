**01 review (web-review pass, desktop/768/390/360 rendered), second-pass candidates, not yet fixed:**
1. Must fix before reuse on valor-ops.com: the frost hides the deliverables, which are purchase-deciding information, and on phones nothing signals the pane is tappable. Glass should conceal secondary detail (evidence, methodology), never what the visitor is deciding on. Add an in-glass hint on touch or move the frost to the sample finding.
2. Should fix: "Inspect" does not say what happens ("Show what you receive"), and the pill buttons break the card radius scale (14px cards, 999px buttons). Use one radius family.
3. Should fix: large prices in Geist Mono read as "$1 , 500" because of the monospaced comma. Set big amounts in Archivo with tabular-nums; keep mono for durations and labels.

**Dillan's verdict on 01 (2026-11-19, live review):** the flat black ground is fine, but the textured cards still read flat. Glass is rejected for this use: a potential client will not uncover what is being offered. Rule for the skill: never put purchase-deciding information behind glass or any interaction.
